Golden Gate Fields closed to racing in June 2024. In February 2026 the Trust for Public Land took an option to buy the 161-acre property for $175 million, about 31 acres of it open water along the shoreline. Funding includes $125 million from the state, $20 million from EBRPD's Measure WW and about $30 million from private donors. *(Report p. 10)*

**What happens next.** EBRPD expects to own and manage the park, take title in spring 2027, run a public planning process from 2027 to 2029, and build in phases as funding allows. *(p. 10)*

**Why water access needs to be in the record now.** The report notes that more public shoreline doesn't guarantee access to the water. Launch areas and parking have to be in the planning record before the landscape is designed. With paid parking proposed at Berkeley, Toll Plaza closed, Crown Beach losing sand and Racetrack already at capacity, the report argues that the new park should be planned around regional demand. The report also recommends a seat for SFBA in EBRPD's planning process. *(pp. 10–11)*
